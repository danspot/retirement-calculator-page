Assumptions

Microsoft stock

All stock vest proceeds are reduced by the stock tax rate (40% by default). Changing the price scales all remaining and future stock values. Grants vest on December 1, March 1, June 1, and September 1 over five years. Under 55/15, post-retirement vesting continues only for grants at least 12 months old.

Healthcare

Employer coverage is free while working. COBRA runs for the configured period, followed by the out-of-pocket amount through September 30, 2032. The matrix shows cumulative costs through both September 2, 2030 and the terminal date.
